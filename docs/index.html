Four Bitcoin-themed handheld LCD games for your phone — grey reflective LCD, segments that cast a shadow, round rubber buttons, piezo beeps, a clock with ДП/ПП and a world Top 5. Original games, original art.

The games

Each game has its own address — share a link straight to your favourite.

BTC MINER

Four ASIC miners roll ₿ coins down cable trays. Catch them in the bucket before they drop. Bull in the window = a miss counts only half; the bear runs off with dropped coins.

4 corner buttons+1 per coinPlay /btc →

SATOSHI'S KITCHEN

The chef flips coins in a pan. Every catch splits the coin smaller until it is 1 sat — cash it out for +5. The bear steals with a fork, the mouse takes what drops.

◀ ▶₿ → satsPlay /kitchen →

NOT YOUR KEYS

Dive from the COLD WALLET boat to the seabed vault and bring the coins home. The Rug-Pull Squid's five tentacles freeze your funds — even at the vault.

◀ ▶+1 grab · +3 bankPlay /keys →

TO THE MOON

Steer the rocket across three lanes, dodge falling FUD clouds and grab bitcoins on the way up. Every layout is guaranteed to have a way through.

◀ ▶altitude + ₿Play /moon →
